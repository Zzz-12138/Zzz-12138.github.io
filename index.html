I am a Ph.D. candidate in Cyberspace Security at Nanjing University of Science and Technology. My research focuses on the implementation security of post-quantum cryptography, particularly side-channel analysis of lattice-based signatures on embedded platforms.

My work spans leakage modeling, public-template and non-profiling attacks, experimental validation, and security evaluation. I am interested in translating implementation-level observations into reproducible attacks and practical guidance for more secure cryptographic systems.
